## Project Overview: 
Flow Tech Plumbing & Drains fixes the community, fixes blocked drains, fixes damaged kitchen sinks, and it has an online website which showcases how it operates. The website has a schedule which has an information of our working days. The prices of repairs. 
## Website Goals and Objectives:
The website will help our business grow, and get a wider reach of customers. From time to time we have specials that we advertise online so that our customers can save money for specials. Our marketing strategy shows unity, and we provide the best service which attracts customers due to online reviews about our work.
## Key Features and Functionality: 
The website will contain a Home page, About page, Services, Enquiry page, Contact page. 
The homepage will introduce what Flow Tech Plumbing & Drains does and show images.
The menu will display product photographs, descriptions, prices, and available options.  

The enquiry page will allow customers to submit custom cake requirements, preferred dates, and contact details.
