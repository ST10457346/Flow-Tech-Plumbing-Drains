Our History

Established in 2018 by a master plumber bringing over 15 years of industry experience, FlowTech Plumbing & Drains originated as a single-van service committed to assisting local residents with urgent repairs. Dedicated service and positive community feedback propelled our ongoing growth.

Mission Statement

To deliver high-grade plumbing solutions with complete integrity, safeguarding community comfort through transparent practices and attentive care.

Vision Statement

To become the region’s most reliable, eco-conscious plumbing service, recognized for practical innovation and local engagement.
